# Roxy Tailor — interface

## Direction

White workbench. Color lives on numbers, stages, and actions — not on the canvas.

## Type

Lato body. Poppins headings (`font-display`).

## Surfaces

White page, white cards, light gray `seam` borders. No beige fills.

## Color on content

- Buttons / primary actions: `action` blue `#2563eb`
- Nav and tabs: `tab` teal `#0f766e` (never the same as buttons)
- Cutting orange, Ready blue, Sewing violet, Done green
- Money: pending orange, collected green
- Ticket codes stay ink, not a brand-red wash

## Flow

Shop: Home · Orders · Staff pay
Admin: Home · Customers · Staff · Cloth types · Money
